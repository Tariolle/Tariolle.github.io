I am an engineering student in Computer Science and AI at [INSA Rouen Normandy](https://www.insa-rouen.fr/) and an incoming research-oriented M.Sc. student in Data Science at the [University of Rouen Normandy](https://mastersd.univ-rouen.fr/home.php). I am interested in deep learning broadly, especially representation learning, world models, deep reinforcement learning, and adversarial machine learning.

My current internships apply deep learning to different domains: at [InterDigital](https://www.interdigital.com/video-lab/video-codec-research), I work on methods for next-generation video compression, with emphasis on VVC (H.266) in-loop filtering and decoder-side filter selection; at [Enedis](https://www.enedis.fr/), I work on generative modeling and PySpark pipelines for high-frequency electricity-grid time-series simulation.

Recent projects include [Opportunistic Target Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25663), a lightweight wrapper for query-efficient score-based black-box adversarial attacks accepted at CAp 2026, and [SLS-WM](https://tariolle.github.io/sls-wm/), an independent research project on structured label smoothing for discrete world models.

I am seeking a six-month final-year research internship in deep learning starting in 2027.
